Go to https://cdnjs.com/, search font awesome and click it. In font awesome copy the following url:
"https://cdnjs.cloudflare.com/ajax/libs/font-awesome/7.3.1/css/all.min.css" and paste in home.php file.

Go to "https://swiperjs.com/", click "Get started" and go to "Use Swiper from CDN" and copy the following link:
"<link
  rel="stylesheet"
  href="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/swiper@12/swiper-bundle.min.css"
/>".
Then paste in home.php file.

Go to "https://swiperjs.com/get-started", find "Use Swiper from CDN" and copy the script (after copied link):
"<script src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/swiper@12/swiper-bundle.min.js"></script>" and paste in home.php file.
